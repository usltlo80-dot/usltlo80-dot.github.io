LTLO Analytics: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who uses this app

LTLO Analytics is an internal tool used only by the owner of Left the Light On to read statistics for the YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account that Left the Light On owns. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the account owner's permission, the app reads, without changing anything:

  • From YouTube (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, read-only scopes): channel details, the list of our uploaded videos, and aggregated statistics such as views, engaged views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, audience country, age and gender, and subscriber counts.
  • From Facebook and Instagram (Graph API: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, read_insights, instagram_basic, instagram_manage_insights): our Page's and our Instagram account's follower counts and aggregated Page, Reel and account insights.

The statistics are aggregated by the platforms. The app does not access viewers' names, contact details, messages, comments or any other personal data of viewers.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to produce weekly performance reports for our own channel. Access tokens and reports are stored on our own computer. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Use of YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; Facebook and Instagram access can be removed in Facebook Settings > Business integrations. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and we delete them.
